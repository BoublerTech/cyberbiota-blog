Project history

The journey

AriseOne grew into a broader CyberBiota homelab and then into CyberBiota3. This timeline keeps the experiments, reversals and gaps visible.

January–February 2026

AriseOne

Foundations

Early inventory, health scripts, Ansible updates, tickets and a growing Proxmox service environment. A December 2025 folder date records archival organization; it does not establish the lab's start date.

Basis: Historical repository and retrospective
February–March 2026

CyberBiota on Proxmox

Experimentation

Service onboarding, monitoring, media workflows and network experiments produced both useful integrations and failures that later shaped the architecture.

Basis: Dated legacy log and retrospectives
April–May 2026

CyberBiota 2

Transition

Records describe retiring the Proxmox iteration, initializing a native Ubuntu/Docker host, and removing n8n before it appeared again in a later platform build. Data continuity still needs proof.

Basis: Dated log and transition summary
June–July 2026

CyberBiota3 foundations

Operating practice

A formal change record, deployment retries, scoped backup and restore evidence, monitoring, and application integration became part of the operating method.

Basis: Repository commits, change results and selected receipts
August–October 2026

CyberBiota3 operations

Acceptance and correction

The records track observability changes, identity and ingress issues, local AI experiments, and stronger rules for interpreting success, failure and incomplete acceptance.

Basis: Dated operations and incident records
November 22–25, 2026

CyberBiota3.5

Host repairs and production cutover

The upgrade repaired time synchronization and container limits, moved Docker storage, added an encrypted external backup, tested scoped operations roles, and put the hybrid coordinator into production. The observation period and documented recovery gaps remained open.

Basis: Dated discovery, validation, restore, and cutover records; final accepted packets on November 25.
How dates are used

A folder creation date, retrospective account, commit, and tested result answer different questions. The earliest located archive metadata is from December 2025; it does not prove the lab began then.

Names such as “CyberBiota 2” are presented as labels in historical records. They do not imply a formal product release.
