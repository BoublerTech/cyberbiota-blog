A new chapter · November 22–25, 2026

The CyberBiota3.5 upgrade

The same home lab, with work to do before it could take on more. Follow the repairs, tests, decisions, and production changes across ten posts.

I started this lab to use open-source technology to host my own apps and data. In November, we worked on the machine behind those services: its crowded system disk, container limits, backup coverage, and the way AI-assisted operations would fit around them.

This series follows the project records in order. The new roles and hybrid coordinator reached production on November 24–25. The Python adapter tests and synthetic drills are described separately from live model tests. Automatic production remediation remained disabled.

As of November 25: the seven-day observation period is still underway. It was scheduled through December 1. Final acceptance, the AIDE integrity-baseline review, and the documented recovery gaps remain open.
