The person behind the lab

My apps. My data.
A lot to learn.

I built and operate CyberBiota as a learning home lab.

Why I started

I wanted to build my first home lab using open-source technology to host my own apps and data. AriseOne was the beginning. I tried earlier versions of CyberBiota, made changes, and eventually wanted a fresh start. CyberBiota3 brings together what I learned along the way.

What I write about

This is my account of running the lab: installing applications, looking after Linux and Docker, connecting services, investigating failures, and checking whether a backup can actually be restored. Some changes work. Some get rolled back. Both belong here.

I write for anyone interested in the work, whether you run a home lab yourself or are just curious about self-hosting.

How I work

I use AI coding assistants for parts of the research, implementation, and editing. I make the project decisions and approve changes. When a test proves only one part of a workflow, I keep that limit in the post. Technical details come from the lab records; I don’t invent missing memories or results to fill a gap.

Old entries describe the lab at the time of the work. They aren’t a live status dashboard.
