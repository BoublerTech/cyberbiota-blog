About & contact

I built and operate CyberBiota3 as a learning homelab.

What this project documents

CyberBiota3's records cover operating services, connecting applications, investigating failures, and checking the results of changes. Its history includes AriseOne and earlier CyberBiota designs, including decisions later revisited.

This portfolio is an account of project work, not a claim of professional tenure, certification or uninterrupted service. Across the case studies and historical pages, the records cover Linux and container administration, network and application troubleshooting, scoped backup testing, monitoring, and change control.

How the work is presented

The records show what was configured or observed at a particular time. Personal interpretation is reviewed separately. AI tools assisted with research, writing or implementation in parts of the project; an AI-generated report or a model's claim is not proof that a workflow succeeded. Where the evidence does not establish an outcome, the portfolio says so.
