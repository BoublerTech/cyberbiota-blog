Questions under consideration

Historical plans include ideas for a later CyberBiota4 generation. Those documents are research inputs, not evidence that new hardware, a cluster or a new application has been deployed.

  • Which limitations can be addressed by simplifying CyberBiota3 before adding another platform?
  • What independent recovery proof would justify changing the storage or backup design?
  • Which automation tasks have clear, safe acceptance criteria and durable receipts?

Future articles will date the decision, alternatives and test outcome if any of these ideas move beyond a proposal.
